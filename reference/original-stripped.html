How it works

From live conversation to intelligent action.

Five stages connect your call to Lily's intelligence and back — in real time.

01 — Connect

Open a copilot session, or start an in-app Mode A call. Guests join Mode A by room code or browser guest link after a consent notice. Regular cellular calls are not captured — Lily works through the app and your phone's microphone.

02 — Listen

Your voice travels over an encrypted connection to Lily's private platform, where speech is transcribed as you talk (English and Hindi/Hinglish).

03 — Understand

Lily follows the flow of the conversation while your profile, playbook, client notes and knowledge are brought together as context for every reply.

04 — Assist

Mode A: the reply is spoken aloud in the call. Mode B: the assistance stays on your screen as private text. An interruption stops the reply in progress, in both modes.

05 — Capture

The conversation is stored in your history with a summary, action items and a follow-up draft — exportable, and deletable by you.

About

Conversations are where work actually happens.

Lily AI exists because the most important moments in business — the negotiation, the interview, the difficult client call — happen live, spoken, and unscripted.

Designed for live conversation

Boardrooms, interview tables, support floors — where important conversations actually happen. Illustrative photo.

The room is where Lily works

Lily is built for the live, spoken, unscripted moment — not the write-up afterwards. Every design choice starts from the conversation itself: listen first, understand in context, assist in the moment, and capture what matters before it fades.

That is why the copilot stays private, why the agent announces itself, and why unfinished capabilities carry honest labels on this site.

Vision

A world where no one walks into an important conversation alone — where preparation, memory and your best phrasing are present in the moment, not reconstructed afterwards.

Mission

Build real-time conversational intelligence that is personal (it works the way you work), private (private by design, consent-led), and honest about what it can and cannot do.

Philosophy

The AI announces itself. The copilot never speaks for you without you. Interruptions are respected, not talked over. And unfinished capabilities are labelled as such — Mode A carries its Beta label on this site because that is the truth of its verification status.

Where it's heading

Live verification of the Mode A voice agent, telephony through a carrier trunk when credentials are in place, and deeper personalization — each shipped only when it has actually been tested.

Questions, answered honestly.

Legal

Privacy Policy

The product's plain-language privacy policy, as it appears in the Lily AI app. A lawyer review is still needed before commercial launch.

What Lily processes

Lily processes the conversation audio, transcripts, summaries and account information needed to provide live copilot and calling features, on the private Lily platform your workspace uses. No outside AI company receives your audio or transcripts.

Consent & retention

Features run only with explicit consent. Conversation data is retained for 30 days (EU) or 90 days (other regions) under the product's per-region consent design, and guests joining a call see a consent notice first.

Your controls

  • Export your workspace data to a portable file on your phone.
  • Delete a workspace you own, which removes its data.
  • Delete your account (available when you no longer own a workspace).

Security

All communication uses encrypted connections. Passwords and sign-in credentials are stored in protected, unreadable form. Keep your device and account credentials secure.

Terms & Conditions

The product's plain-language terms, as presented in the Lily AI app. A lawyer review is still needed before commercial launch.

Using Lily AI

You are responsible for using Lily lawfully and for obtaining any consent your region requires before involving others in an AI-assisted conversation. In Mode A, Lily announces that it is an AI at the start of a call.

Current limitations

  • Mode A voice calling is in Beta and is provided for in-app conversations.
  • Lily does not provide access to ordinary mobile call audio, and calling regular phone numbers requires a separate carrier arrangement.
  • AI suggestions and summaries can be wrong — review them before acting.

Your content

Your playbooks, knowledge and conversation history remain yours. You can export or delete them using the in-app controls described in the Privacy Policy.
