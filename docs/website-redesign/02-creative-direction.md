# 02 — Creative direction

## Positioning
- **Brand:** Lily AI. **Primary line:** Your identical twin on every call. **Supporting line:** Listening. Thinking. Briefing you live.
- **Concept:** "The Intelligence Between Every Conversation" — one luminous core, two signal paths.

## The visual idea
A single procedural **conversational core** stands for Lily. Everything else on the site follows from it.

| Metaphor | How it appears |
|---|---|
| Luminous conversational core | Noise-displaced glass shell with a live waveform inside |
| Living sound field | Rings whose radius carries a travelling waveform |
| Two synchronized signal paths | **Mode A, cyan**, leaves the core and travels outward to the other participant. **Mode B, lavender**, leaves the core and loops back toward the viewer, because it is private to the owner |
| Context in orbit | Small nodes orbiting the core (voice, playbook, history, knowledge) |

The two colours are a rule, not decoration: **cyan always means "spoken, Mode A"** and **lavender always means "private, Mode B"** — in badges, frames, diagrams, navigation and the 3D scene.

## Two explorations considered

| | A. Signal Midnight (chosen) | B. Editorial Daylight |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Midnight navy, cinematic | Ice white, magazine-like |
| 3D | Glowing core with additive light | Frosted glass core on white |
| Strength | Light reads as signal; the two mode colours stay distinct; closest to the brand's existing orb imagery | Very readable, calm, unusual for an AI product |
| Weakness | Risk of a generic "dark AI site" | Additive glow and signal paths lose their impact on white; cyan and lavender fail contrast as text |

**Decision: A, with B's strength borrowed.** Dark cinematic sections carry the 3D and the product demos. Bright editorial sections (problem, how it works, use cases, security, legal) carry the reading. Alternating them gives the page rhythm and avoids the all-dark look.

## What was avoided
Robot heads, AI brains, headset stock photos as hero art, purple-gradient SaaS styling, gradients behind every heading, and repeated identical card grids.

## Decisions on existing assets
- **The three embedded films were removed.** They appear AI-generated and their on-screen text is garbled, and the old site presented them as Lily "in action". They are replaced by coded interface simulations, each labelled "Illustrative demo — not a live recording". The original files remain inside the source HTML.
- **Stock photos kept** for the role explorer and About, with the existing "models, not customers" disclaimer.
- **Logo kept unchanged.** A transparent PNG was derived from the JPEG and placed on a white tile so the mark renders as designed. Redrawing it is an owner decision.

## MotionSites references
Part 1 of the MotionSites library (324 of 648 entries) was read through the MCP on the free plan. Entries closest to this brief: `digital-persona`, `ai-companions`, `glass-orb`, `futuristic-cinematic`, `iris-assistant`, `ai-meeting-notes`, `global-gateway`. Only their one-line descriptions were used, as principles: one hero object, a restrained palette, glass product panels over a dark field. No prompt, template or asset was fetched or copied. Part 2 of the library was not read.
